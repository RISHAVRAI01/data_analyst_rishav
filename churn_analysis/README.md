# Supply Chain Analytics

## Project Overview
Analyzed a supply chain dataset to understand revenue performance, inventory levels, supplier performance, product quality, shipping costs, transportation, and operational risk.

## Business Questions
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which suppliers contribute the most revenue?
- Which SKUs have low inventory availability?
- Which products have high defect rates?
- What is the inspection failure rate?
- Which shipping carriers have the lowest average shipping costs?
- Which transportation modes generate the most revenue?
- Which locations and routes perform best?
- Which SKUs have the highest supply-chain risk?

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue: ₹577.60K
- Total Units Sold: 46.70K
- Average Defect Rate: 2.28%
- Inspection Fail Rate: 36.00%
- Average Total Cycle Time: 37.60
- Low Availability Records: 18
- Low Stock/Order Ratio Records: 24
- High Defect Records: 31

## Key Insights
- Skincare was the highest-revenue product category.
- Supplier 1 was the strongest revenue contributor.
- A significant number of records had low inventory availability or low stock-to-order ratios.
- 31 records had defect rates above 3%.
- The 36% inspection failure rate highlighted a significant quality-control concern.
- Shipping carriers showed differences in average shipping costs.
- SKU-level risk analysis identified products requiring priority attention.
