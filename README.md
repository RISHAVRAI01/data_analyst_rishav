### 3. E-Commerce Sales Analysis

Analyzed Brazilian e-commerce sales data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, payment methods, product categories, delivery performance, and customer reviews.

**Tools & Technologies:**
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Power BI

**Key Areas:**
- Sales and revenue analysis
- Customer analysis
- Product category performance
- Payment method analysis
- Geographic analysis
- Delivery performance
- Customer review analysis
- Repeat customer behavior

**Key KPIs:**

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹15.84M |
| Total Orders | 98.67K |
| Average Order Value | ₹160.58 |
| Total Customers | 95.42K |
| Repeat Customer Rate | 3.05% |

**Key Insights:**
- Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue.
- Credit cards accounted for the majority of payment revenue.
- São Paulo was a major customer market.
- Late deliveries were associated with lower review scores.
- Most customers were one-time purchasers.

 **Project:** [`e-commerce`](./e-commerce)
