# Olist E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
Analyzed the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset to understand sales performance, customer behavior, payment methods, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction.

## Business Questions
- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which customer states contribute the most sales?
- What payment methods are most commonly used?
- How do delivery delays affect customer ratings?
- What proportion of customers are repeat customers?

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue: ₹15.84M
- Total Orders: 98.67K
- Average Order Value: ₹160.58
- Total Customers: 95.42K
- Repeat Customer Rate: 3.05%

## Key Insights
- Health & Beauty was the highest-revenue category.
- Credit cards generated the majority of payment revenue.
- São Paulo was the strongest customer-state market.
- Late deliveries were associated with substantially lower review scores.
- Most customers were one-time purchasers.
